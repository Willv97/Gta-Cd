About this site

This is a single-purpose fan page that counts down, live, to the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI on November 19, 2026.

  • The clock updates every second and shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds left, plus the total hours to go.
  • It uses your device's clock and time zone, so it counts to midnight where you are, not a single worldwide moment.
  • On release day the tiles are replaced by a launch message.
  • Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. The page only reads your device's time.

It is an unofficial project, made by a fan, with original artwork. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
